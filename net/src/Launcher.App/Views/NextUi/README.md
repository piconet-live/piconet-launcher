# Launcher.App/Views/NextUi

The redesigned interface's views: what the window shows with Settings' "Experimental UI" on (Launcher, "Try something
new"; off by default). They stand beside the classic views in `..`, on the same view models (`../../ViewModels`, and
`../../ViewModels/NextUi` for what is new), and replace none of them: `../MainWindow.axaml.cs` puts `NextShell` in the
classic interface's place, and `../../ViewLocator.cs` (`Next`) gives a page its view from here, or the classic one
while it has none here yet. Their look is theme v3 (`../../Theme/NextUi/Theme.axaml`); no view sets a colour.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `NextShell.axaml` | The shell: the background (`Controls/NextUi/HoloFlag`) with its veil and shades, and on the hub Pico's Wild Goose over the flag, its engines burning, filmed shot after shot as an advertisement is, in the middle of the room right of the hub's menu; the pointer turns and holds it, let go it goes on a few seconds later (`../../Controls/MachinePreview.cs`, `Showcase`, `RoomAfter`; from the player's disc); the top bar (the launcher's name, always at the top left: the main menu, `HubViewModel.GoHome`; Back's place beside it, kept whether Back shows or not; the areas between their keys, the states' pills, the count of the players online (only shown), the profile, the bell, the chat, Settings, the window's own buttons), which is also what the window is dragged by; the server's notice under it; the page; the hint bar; the bell's list and the chat (the classic launcher's); a panel from the right edge (`Shell.Sheet`); the band that calls the player back from a practice (with "Back now"); behind a page that stands on a course (the course drawn, the start, the race), that course's picture from the disc, darkened (the owner's decision 4); the questions (the shell's one question, `Shell.Ask`; before a lobby is left for the game; before ray tracing); a tip on each of the top bar's buttons and areas. `.axaml.cs`: the keys that do what the pad's buttons do (Q, E, Escape, X the chat, Y the page's second action, S; Ctrl and the arrows through a page's sub-tabs as R1; the mouse's side buttons back and forward), which of the two the hints show, the background's wave and the page's entrance at a change, the focus while a question shows |
| `HubView.axaml` | Play's first page, the hub: the server's message of the day in a place of its own (pressed: its panel), the four entries with the line of the one the focus is on, whether the player is online, the way to the lobbies and how many race now; the room right of them left to the shell's showcase (`Menu` is where it begins); while a game runs, "End game" (it asks first) |

| `ServerSheetView.axaml` | The panel a server's row opens, one step at a time: three like cards, each its title alone, centred in the display face: JOIN A RACE there (Quick Play: an open Quick Play lobby, else "No race found"), HOST A LOBBY there (a Quick Play lobby, never a custom one: those are the hub's Custom lobby), SEE ITS LOBBIES; what each does in its tip; and Close; the focus on the first |

| `RulesPageView.axaml` | A custom lobby's rules as an option page in steps (the lobby, the race, on request More rules; the trail over the rows): the step's rows on the left, on the right what the focused row does (a mode or a stats file from a file, the course's picker), NEXT then the page's action, More rules on the race's step, Back a step back; a fixed course chosen in a panel over the page; a step puts the focus on its first row |
| `CustomListView.axaml` | The custom lobbies: a row a lobby (lock, name, host and rules, how full, what it is doing), the password asked over the page, Create lobby (in Quick Play: Leave Quick Play); Create as `RulesPageView` |
| `RoomView.axaml`, `HostRulesView.axaml` | A custom lobby while it gathers: its name, host and rules, a tile a racer (the host presses one to remove them, after a question), the rules and All rules; the host's Edit rules (`HostRulesView`, the option page) and Start race; the others' vote to start; a team; "Practice while you wait" |
| `RulesSheetView.axaml` | Every rule of a custom lobby, as rows that say their value (those that differ from Quick Play's marked) |
| `GaragePageView.axaml` | The Garage (the area's page, and a lobby's whole Garage) in two steps. The machines: the shelves as chips, the search, the stats' lens, Sort & filter with its dot; at the left the shelf's machines as a grid of four that scrolls up and down, New machine last on Custom; the machine under the focus large with "In use" and its grades. A tile pressed: the machine's own page, the machine large, and the card (grades, All stats, the setting that left and right step, its colours and "+", Use this machine, the star; a custom machine's Edit parts and Remove); only Use this machine races it, and goes back to the machines; B too. The builder shows in its place. `.axaml.cs`: a step takes the focus with it (Use this machine, or the machine's tile) |
| `BuilderPageView.axaml` | The builder as an option page in two steps, the parts and the pilot, then the colours, as rows (`OptionList`) under the trail; the machine in 3D with its name and grades, what the row does, NEXT then Save machine, and Back a step back |
| `SortSheetView.axaml`, `ColourSheetView.axaml`, `StatsSheetView.axaml` | The Garage's panels: Sort & filter; the colours in two steps (the palette, then "Mix your own colour": the wheel and the hex, B back to the palette); every value of the machine (a stock machine's rows sort by their stat) |

| `NextShell.axaml` (KeyboardLayer) | The keyboard on the screen, over everything: a text box the pad presses opens it (`../../Controls/PadNav.cs`, Typing), Done puts the text in the box |

| `ProfileView.axaml`, `NameSheetView.axaml` | The profile, and its Change name |

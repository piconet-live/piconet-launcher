# Launcher.App/ViewModels/NextUi

What the redesigned interface ("Experimental UI"; off by default) shows and does that the classic one has no page
for. Everything else is the classic launcher's view models in `..`, called from here and never copied.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `ShellViewModel.cs` | The shell: the areas (Play, Garage, Replays, More; Settings the fifth) and the page last shown in each, the way back from a page inside one (Back and B: the same way; on a page gone through in steps, a step back first: the Garage's machine page, Practice's set-up, a lobby's rules, the builder's colours, the colours' mix), the panel open over the page, the hints for the page showing (the lobby's by its phase), the lobby's pill, the band that calls the player back from a practice, the course's picture behind a lobby's page; a custom lobby's rules pages, whether a page fits the room it has; the Garage's page; About and the profile (Back to where it was opened from); whether a race can be joined (`CanJoin`: the panel that prepares the game's files, or the reason with OK; RACE, the servers, the lobbies and the custom lobbies ask it, no press does nothing); the one question before what cannot be taken back (`Ask`: a lobby or a race left, a racer removed, a custom machine, a replay or a mod deleted, the game ended), gone with its matter; the Settings and Controller pages, the keyboard on the screen, the pad's look (its icons' family, the confirm button); the first start (Back a step) |
| `ServersViewModel.cs` | Quick Play's first page, "Choose a server": this build's official server and the player's own (`LauncherSettings.Servers`), each asked of its state only while the page shows, with its ping; a press opens the panel that asks what to do there (`ServerSheetViewModel.cs`); a server added or removed (the owner's decision 7) |
| `ServerSheetViewModel.cs` | A server's three ways, one step at a time: join a race there (Quick Play: its fullest open lobby, else "No race found" and nothing joined), host a lobby there (a Quick Play lobby: Quick Play's queue, `PlayControlViewModel.HostAsync`; never a custom lobby, which is the hub's Custom lobby), or see its lobbies (the lobbies page of that server) |
| `JoinCheck.cs` | The check before a race is joined (RACE, a server, a lobby, a custom lobby): from a custom lobby the question before it is left (and the join goes on; for another custom lobby or Create, on the same visit: the list stays), game files that are not ready the panel that prepares them, any other reason said with OK; no press does nothing. RACE and "Join a race" join an open Quick Play lobby only (`PlayControlViewModel.NoOpenLobby`): with none open "No race found", OK alone (`NoRace`) |
| `HubViewModel.cs` | Play's first page, the hub: Quick Play, Custom lobby, Practice, Start F-Zero GX (Prepare the game while the game's files are not ready: it, Quick Play and Practice open the panel that prepares them), and the line for the one the focus is on; the top bar's count of the players online and its tip (only shown, never pressed); the server's message of the day and "Hide for today"; the way to it from anywhere (the top bar's name: `GoHome`, which asks first where something would be lost or missed: a new lobby's rules, the host's unsaved rules, a server's address being typed, a button being set, any lobby or the queue, a game running) |
| `SheetViewModels.cs` | The panels the shell opens from the window's right edge: the lobby's, the race's racers, the message of the day, every rule of a custom lobby (each only names what it shows: the lobby page's, the play control's and the hub's own) |
| `OptionItem.cs` | An option row: one of the classic rule rows (`../RuleRows.cs`) with its value in words, its help line and its group; what left, right and A do to each kind of row (`OptionSteps`: a choice stops at its ends, A steps on to the end, never round; `Room` says which way is left), and the help lines (`RuleHelp`) |
| `SwatchRule.cs` | A colour row: left and right go through the colours a part or the trail has (the paint last), A opens every colour |
| `GaragePageViewModel.cs` | The Garage (the area's page, a lobby's whole Garage) in two steps, the machines and a machine's own page (`Detail`; B a step back, a visit begins with the machines): the card of the machine on the stage (its grades, its setting stepped by five, its colours, "Use this machine", the star, a custom machine's Edit parts and Remove), the grid's focus and press (a machine on the stage, never raced: only "Use this machine" races it), the shelves, the lens, the panels it opens, the builder's page; what it shows and keeps is the classic Garage's (`../GarageViewModel.Stage.cs`) |
| `BuilderPageViewModel.cs` | The builder as an option page (New machine, Edit machine) in two steps: the parts and the pilot, then each part's colour and the trail's, the machine's name and grades, Save machine (kept and raced) and Back (a change kept as the classic builder's draft) |
| `DiagnosticsSheetViewModel.cs` | Developer mode's Diagnostics (its DEV chip): the build, the files, the connection, the input, the interface; Copy diagnostics, Open the log, Open the data folder, Show the launch file, Turn off |
| `DevKeys.cs` | Developer mode's keys at the rows: a setting's launch file key, a lobby rule's key, the launcher's own settings |
| `PadLooks.cs` | The pad's look (its icons' family, the confirm button) and a hint's button in it (a GameCube controller's in its own colours and sizes: `Hint.Cube`); Developer mode's two lines in the hint bar (`DevLines`) |
| `OptionRows.cs` | Option rows that work a value held elsewhere by a getter and a setter (`IOptionRow`): a choice, a switch, a number, a row pressed to do something; each with whether it can be worked and its line under it |
| `SettingsPageViewModel.cs`, `SettingInfo.cs` | Settings in steps, as the game's options are: the groups first (Basics with the six rows, then the eight others, each with what is in it), a group open showing its option rows, B back to the groups; the list on the left half, on the right a window on the group or row the focus is on (its drawing, also large behind it, what it does, its value now, every choice with the one chosen marked or a number's meter, when a change takes effect and what it applies to, something good to know: `SettingFacts.cs`; how it is changed; a group's rows with their values); the Sound group's rows by kind (the game's music and sounds, the launcher's, its menu sounds, notifications and other sounds); every row the classic page's own setting (`../SettingsViewModel.cs`); Graphics as one choice over three settings (`GraphicsPresets`) |
| `ControllerPageViewModel.cs` | Settings › Controller as option rows: the port, its input and device, the button icons and the confirm button, rumble, the game's own set-up, the dead zones, the buttons and their reset (`../ControllerViewModel.cs`) |
| `KeyboardViewModel.cs` | The keyboard on the screen: what is typed (a password's as dots), the keys, Shift, Space, Delete, Done and Cancel |
| `FirstStartViewModel.cs` | The first start in three steps (the disc image and the game prepared, the name, the controller), Next, Back and Let's race; everything the classic first screen's (`../DiscViewModel.cs`: its checks, its preparation, every sentence it says) |
| `AboutViewModel.cs` | More › About: the version, the update and its action, "Update automatically" and "Developer mode" as option rows (the Settings page's own switches), "What's new" (this version's release notes), "Licences" |
| `ProfileViewModel.cs` | The profile (the top bar's chip): the name and how races show it, the dot and the server, Change name and Server, the account (later), the machine raced, three figures, the last three races to watch; everything the classic launcher's (`../AccountViewModel.cs`, `../StatsViewModel.cs`, `../ReplaysViewModel.cs`) |
| `MoreSheets.cs` | The panels of Replays, More and the profile: a replay's (its facts, Play, Results, the mode file, Show in folder, Delete), a mod's (on, the order, its stat pack, its parts, Remove), the licences, the name; a panel that belongs to one page (`IOwnedSheet`) |
| `PrepareSheetViewModel.cs` | The panel that prepares the game's files wherever the launcher finds them missing, damaged or another version's after its first start: the first screen's work (`../DiscViewModel.cs`, one of its own), closed once the game is ready; whether the files are wanted, and their state in a word for Settings |
| `GarageSheets.cs` | The Garage's panels: Sort & filter (the stats, the way round, the letters, Clear the filter, a lobby's "All 41"), the colours in two steps (what is coloured, its own colours, twelve to paint with, Back to the original; then Mix your own colour: hex and the wheel), All stats; a panel's step back (`IGarageSheet.StepBack`) |
| `RulesPageViewModel.cs` | A custom lobby's rules as an option page (Create, and the host's Edit rules) in steps, a few rows a screen: the lobby (name, password, racers), the race (mode, course, the mode's own), on request More rules; NEXT, then the page's action; B a step back; the row the focus is on; the rows are the classic editor's (`../LobbyRulesEditor.cs`) |
| `MenuViewModel.cs` | A page that is a short list of places to go into: More (Mods, Stats), and Settings' four pages until Settings has its one page |
